# Follow List — the ~25 accounts that matter, and what to do with them

> **The principle: following is not the point. Being seen is the point.**
> A list of accounts you read is just a nicer feed. What gets you hired is that some of these people have seen your name three or four times before they ever see your application. That takes about 20 minutes a week, and it only works if you're shipping something (`ai-journey-90-days`). Names without receipts is networking. Receipts plus names is a pipeline.

**Hard rule, from the plan:** every account you follow, every reply you post, every DM you send — **personal phone or home computer only. Never the work machine.** This starts now, not in October.

## Tier 3 — Rooms, not people

Where questions get answered and where jobs get posted before they hit a job board.

**Reddit** — [r/LLMDevs](https://www.reddit.com/r/LLMDevs/) is the one that matches you: RAG pipelines, agent orchestration, production problems. [r/LocalLLaMA](https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/) is the highest-signal AI subreddit overall but skews to local/hardware — read, don't chase. Skip r/artificial and r/singularity entirely; they will make you dumber and they will eat your mornings.

**Discord** — the [Latent Space Discord](https://www.latent.space/p/community) is the closest thing to a single room where applied AI engineers actually are. Jobs get posted there. Join it, mute everything, check the jobs channel weekly.

**Hacker News** — the monthly **"Ask HN: Who is hiring?"** thread (first working day of each month, [news.ycombinator.com](https://news.ycombinator.com/)). Ctrl-F for `REMOTE` + `TypeScript` + `LLM` or `agent`. Fifteen minutes, once a month, and it is one of the few places small AI startups post before they build a careers page.

**YC job board** — [ycombinator.com/jobs](https://www.ycombinator.com/jobs). Filter for AI + remote. Firecrawl, Morph, Continue and Browser Use are all YC; when they open a role it appears here first.

**In person** — [AI Tinkerers](https://aitinkerers.org/) has chapters across LatAm. Check whether Buenos Aires has one. One demo night where you show your day-NN experiment is worth two months of replies on X.

## The protocol — 20 minutes a week, Friday

Fridays are already mock-interview day in `learning-system.md`. Bolt this on to the end of it, on your phone, so it never competes with a build slot.

**1. Three replies (10 min).** Not "great post 🔥". A reply that costs you something: a number from your own experiment, a disagreement with a reason, a question only someone who tried it would ask. *"Ran this on Russian text and got a 2.8x token ratio vs English — matches what you'd expect from BPE merges on an English-heavy corpus"* is a reply that gets you a follow back. Three per week, from Tier 1 first.

**2. One receipt (10 min).** Post one thing from `ai-journey-90-days`. A screenshot of the tokenizer output. The RU:EN ratio you measured. One paragraph on why you chose a hand-rolled state machine over LangGraph. It does not need to be clever — it needs to exist and be dated. Thirty of these by October is a portfolio that no CV can fake.

**3. Once a month: the scan (15 min).** HN "Who is hiring", the YC board, the Latent Space jobs channel. Log anything real in `interview-log.md` — which, right now, is still empty.

**What you do not do:** cold DM anyone before you have receipts. A DM that says "I'm learning AI, are you hiring?" is a delete. A DM that says "I've been building agents in TS for three months — here's the repo, here's a thing I found about your tool" is a conversation. **The reply-and-ship loop is what earns the right to the DM.** Expect that to be around week 6, not week 1.

---

## Why this list is short

You have a starting problem, not an information problem. Two hundred follows would feel like progress on Saturday and be a muted, unread feed by Wednesday. Twenty-five accounts, three with notifications on, one 20-minute Friday block. If in a month it feels thin, we add. We do not start wide.
