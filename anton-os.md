## Identity (one sentence)

I'm an engineer who ships small things daily, and a man whose family feels his presence — not his plans.

## The Start Rule (your own discovery, in your own words)

Interest arrives 5–7 minutes AFTER starting. Never before. So the only decision I ever make is: **start the first 5 minutes**. Not "do the session", not "be motivated" — just open the file and say "drill". The rest happens on its own. It's not hard to continue. It was never hard to continue. It's only hard to start, and starting is 5 minutes long.

## The four roles — one behavior each. Not ten. One.

**Engineer.** The 1.5-hr slot, weekdays. Say "drill" or open the repo. Bad day = 30 min. Zero is the only failure. This slot IS the Bariloche house, the salary, the self-respect — one thing, wearing four masks.

**Father.** Some daily block of time when the kid gets you with your phone in another room. Presence is measured by the kid, not by you. He won't remember the plans you made for him; he'll remember whether your eyes were on him or on a screen.

**Husband.** You're both carrying a move, a small kid, and a career pivot. Once a week, talk to her about something that is not logistics. The Bariloche plan is FOR her too — make sure she hears about the dream more often than about the tasks.

**Son.** Your parents are far away. One call a week. Twenty minutes. That's the entire job description, and one day it will be the thing you're most glad you did.

## Input diet (feeding the brain right)

- **Default input = builders, not commentators**: Simon Willison, Hamel Husain, Latent Space, Eugene Yan. AI-engineer YouTubers are legal as *ignition* — watch one video to trigger the 5-minute effect, then build. Video that replaces building is sugar.
- **Books**: evenings only, 20–30 min, in English. Currently: So Good They Can't Ignore You.
- The rule that beats all rules: **1 hour of making > 10 hours of watching people make.**

## What a successful day looks like (all of it)

Slot started (even 30 min) · kid got real presence · body moved or was fed decently · one input was a builder, not noise. That's it. A man who does this 5 days out of 7 for a year is unrecognizable — no insight required.

## When it breaks (it will)

Sick kid, deadline, grey day: do the 5-minute version of everything — one revisit question, one hug with attention, one message to your wife, skip the rest without guilt. The system is designed to bend. Quitting quietly is the only real failure, and even then: the restart is always just 5 minutes long.
