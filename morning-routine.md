# Morning Routine — 15 min, English + AI news in one move

> The design principle: **reading AI news in English IS the English practice.** Not two habits — one habit, two outputs. You only have a starting problem, so there is only one thing to start.

## What happens automatically

A brief lands every weekday at **06:30 Buenos Aires time** (push notification). It contains:

1. **Three items** from the last 24-48h — practitioner-relevant only (models, agents, RAG, evals, tooling). No funding rounds, no hype.
2. **A read-aloud passage** — 4-6 sentences, marked for speaking out loud.
3. **Five words or phrases** — vocabulary from today's items, with definitions and example sentences.
4. **A speaking prompt** — one interview-style question to answer out loud for 60 seconds.
5. **A link to your prep** — when an item touches a Core-40 topic you're drilling.

You don't search, you don't choose, you don't decide. That's the point — the choice is what kills mornings.

## What you do (15 min)

**Read the three items** (~5 min). Silently is fine.

**Read the passage out loud** (~2 min). Actually out loud, actual voice. This is the only part that trains the muscle interviews test. Nobody is listening; do it anyway.

**Say the speaking answer out loud** (~3 min). 60 seconds, no notes. Paste it into the session afterward if you want a 0-10 score — some mornings yes, most mornings no. Both are fine.

**Add one phrase to `english-pack.md`** (~2 min). One. Not five. The one you'd actually use in an interview.

**Skim, don't hoard** (~3 min buffer). If an item genuinely grabs you, open the source link. If not, close it and go. Curiosity is fuel, not obligation.

## Bad-morning version (3 min)

Kid woke up early, slept badly, no time: **read the passage out loud, learn one word, close it.** That's a completed morning. Zero is the only failure.

## Rules that keep it from rotting

- **No feeds before the brief.** X, YouTube, Reddit — after, never before. Cheap stimulation first thing raises your threshold and makes real reading feel flat for hours.
- **The brief is input, not homework.** If you skip a day, there's no debt. Tomorrow's brief is fresh.
- **Videos are ignition, not the meal.** An AI-engineer video is a legitimate way to trigger the 5-minute effect — watch one, then go build. A video that replaces building is sugar.
- **If it stops being interesting, say so.** The brief's prompt can be retuned any time — different sources, different depth, shorter. A routine you resent is a routine you'll quit.
